## Standard Foundation Board Meeting Agenda

Use this order as the default agenda structure unless the Chairperson or Board directs otherwise.

1. **Call to Order**
   - Record the meeting date, location, and exact time called to order.
2. **Opening Prayer**
3. **Roll Call / Attendance**
   - List each person present by full name and Foundation title or representative role.
   - Record excused or absent members when known.
   - Note guests separately.
4. **Approval of Agenda**
   - Record amendments, if any.
5. **Approval of Previous Meeting Minutes**
   - Identify the meeting date of the minutes being considered.
   - Record the motion, second, and disposition.
6. **Treasurer's Report / Financial Report**
   - Present balances, investment or account activity, receipts, distributions, and other material financial information as applicable.
   - Record Board actions arising from the report.
7. **Development Director / Development Report**
   - Donor-development activity, communications, planned-giving work, website/material updates, and related initiatives.
8. **Old Business / Unfinished Business**
   - Carry forward unresolved matters from prior meetings.
9. **New Business**
   - New proposals, gifts, policies, distributions, projects, or other matters requiring Board consideration.
10. **Other Business / Announcements**
11. **Next Meeting**
   - Record the agreed or proposed date, time, and location when known.
12. **Adjournment**
   - Record the motion and exact adjournment time.
13. **Closing Prayer**
   - Use when customary or directed by the Chairperson.

## Standardization for Foundation Meeting Minutes

Meeting minutes should be a concise official record of what the Board did, not a transcript of everything said.

### Header

Every set of minutes should identify:

- **St. Peter Lutheran Church Foundation Board of Directors**
- meeting type, if other than a regular meeting;
- date;
- meeting location;
- call-to-order time; and
- adjournment time.

### Attendance

On first reference, use each attendee's **full name and title/role**. Thereafter, use names consistently throughout the minutes.

Separate attendance into:

- Directors present;
- Advisors / ex officio representatives present;
- Directors absent or excused, when known; and
- Guests, when applicable.

### Motions and votes

For each formal action, record:

- the motion;
- the person making the motion;
- the person seconding the motion;
- amendments, if material; and
- whether the motion carried, failed, or was otherwise disposed of.

Do not invent a mover, seconder, vote count, or outcome when the source record does not establish it.

### Discussion

Summarize material discussion only to the degree necessary to understand Board decisions, assignments, or follow-up. Remove speech disfluencies and conversational repetition from transcript-based minutes while preserving substantive meaning.

### Action items

Clearly identify assignments and follow-up items, including the responsible person or committee and deadline when the Board established one.

### Approval status

Minutes prepared before Board approval should be marked **Draft — Subject to Board Approval**. Once approved, retain the approved version as the official record and record any amendments adopted by the Board.

## Information Maintenance

When updating this file:

1. Prefer approved Board minutes, adopted policies, official church/Foundation records, and current Foundation website content as sources.
2. Do not guess at names, titles, contact information, or governance procedures.
3. Update board membership promptly after an election, appointment, resignation, or officer change becomes official.
4. Update the standard agenda when the Board adopts a new recurring order of business.
5. Verify address, phone, and public web information before using this reference for formal correspondence or printed materials.
6. Keep detailed technical repository instructions in `/AGENTS.md`; this file is for Foundation institutional reference information.
